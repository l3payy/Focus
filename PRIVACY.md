# Privacy Policy — Focus

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Focus is a custom new tab page. This policy explains what the extension does with data.

## Data we collect

**Nothing.** Focus has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no servers. We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data, browsing history or user activity.

## Data stored on your device

Everything stays in your browser's local storage and never leaves it:
- links and site names you add (localStorage)
- pictures, notes and drawings on the Cloud board (IndexedDB)
- background images (IndexedDB)
- favicon disk cache (Cache Storage)
- settings: theme, search provider, mail link, toggles (localStorage)

Uninstalling the extension removes this data with the browser profile.

## Network requests

The extension contacts the network only on your explicit actions:
- **Favicons** (`icons.duckduckgo.com`, `ico.faviconkit.net`): downloaded once when you add a site, then served from the local disk cache, including offline
- **Search**: your query is sent to the search engine you selected (Google or Yandex) only when you submit a search

No data is sent anywhere else, at any time.

## Permissions used

- `unlimitedStorage` — prevents the browser from evicting your pictures and caches under storage pressure
- Host access to the two favicon services above — favicon download only

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will ship with the extension update.


Focus не использует аккаунты, собственные серверы, аналитику и рекламу. Ссылки, картинки, заметки, рисунки, настройки и кеш иконок хранятся только в вашем браузере; разработчик их не получает.
