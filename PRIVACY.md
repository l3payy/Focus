# Privacy Policy — Focus (Фокус)

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Focus is a browser extension that replaces the New Tab page. It has no accounts,
no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device
Your saved links, images, notes, drawings, settings and cached site icons are
stored only in your browser (localStorage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage). The
developer does not receive or have access to this data. Removing the extension
deletes it.

## What is sent over the network
- When you add a site, Focus requests that site's icon from third-party icon
  services (icons.duckduckgo.com and www.google.com/s2, which redirects to
  gstatic.com). The request contains the domain of the site you added. The
  icon is then cached locally.
- When you use the search box, your query is sent to the search engine you
  selected (Google or Yandex), under that service's own privacy policy.

Focus does not read or modify web pages you visit and has no content scripts.

## What we do not do
We do not collect, sell or share personal data, and we do not use data for
advertising, profiling or credit decisions.

# Политика конфиденциальности — Focus (Фокус)

Focus не использует аккаунты, собственные серверы, аналитику и рекламу.
Ссылки, картинки, заметки, рисунки, настройки и кеш иконок хранятся только в
вашем браузере; разработчик их не получает. При добавлении сайта домен
отправляется сервисам иконок (DuckDuckGo, Google) для загрузки значка;
поисковые запросы уходят в выбранный вами Google или Яндекс. Данные не
продаются и не передаются для рекламы. Связь: GitHub Issues в репозитории.
